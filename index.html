Engagement terms & scope

Written proposalEach engagement confirms services, period, entity/GSTIN, locations, volumes, deliverables, frequency, timelines and fees.
Monthly retainersPayable in advance on or before the agreed due date unless the proposal states otherwise.
Project assignmentsUnless otherwise agreed, 50% advance on acceptance and 50% before final delivery or completion.
Invoice & taxesInvoice due within 7 calendar days. Fees are exclusive of GST, if applicable. Applicable TDS may be deducted by the client.
Third-party costsGovernment fees, statutory payments, late fees, interest, penalties, software, DSC/e-sign and other third-party costs are extra at actuals.
Scope changesAdditional work, higher volumes, extra GSTINs or locations and changed deliverables require written agreement and may change fees or timelines.
Pause & terminationWork may be paused after reasonable written notice for unpaid undisputed invoices or missing essential inputs. Recurring engagements may be terminated on 30 days’ written notice unless agreed otherwise.
ConfidentialityClient information is handled confidentially for authorised engagement purposes. Banking PINs, card PINs and personal UPI PINs will never be requested.
Important service boundaries: Monthly retainers cover only deliverables expressly listed in the signed proposal. Statutory audit, tax audit, certification, attestation, legal opinion, reserved professional services, representation before tax authorities, litigation, forensic investigation, physical stock verification and data reconstruction are excluded unless separately agreed with an appropriately qualified professional. The client remains responsible for source-document accuracy, business decisions, tax payments, statutory obligations and final review/approval of returns and submissions.
